About

Track your watch list without losing the plot.

SeriesTracker is a web-based watch progress tracker built for anime, donghua, C-dramas, K-dramas, movies, and web series. It helps viewers keep every title, season, episode count, favorite, and upcoming airing reminder in one place.

What SeriesTracker Helps With

  • Save anime, dramas, movies, and web series to a personal tracker.
  • Track watched episodes, completed titles, planned titles, paused titles, and dropped titles.
  • Organize titles by category, status, favorites, and custom sorting.
  • Check upcoming episodes and seasons where schedule data is available.
  • Sync tracker data with a Firebase account.
