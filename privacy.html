Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

SeriesTracker is designed to help users manage watch progress for anime, dramas, movies, and web series. This page explains what information may be collected and how it may be used.

Information You Provide

When you create an account or sign in, SeriesTracker may process account details such as email address, username, password authentication, profile preferences, and tracker entries that you choose to save.

Tracker and Preference Data

Your saved titles, categories, watch status, episode progress, favorites, shared lists, theme settings, and profile preferences may be stored through Firebase services or in your browser's local storage.

Third-Party Services

SeriesTracker may use Firebase for account authentication and cloud sync. It may also request entertainment metadata, images, and airing information from services such as TMDB and AniList. Those services may process requests under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and Cookies

If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit the Contact page.
